A simple mathematics project that demonstrates **fast calculation techniques and shortcut methods** for solving multiplication, squares, square roots, cube roots, and finding the last two digits of powers.

## 🧮 Methods Used

### 1. Split and Solve Method

This method splits numbers into convenient parts to make multiplication easier.

**Example:**

```text
46 × 54
= (50 - 4)(50 + 4)
= 50² - 4²
= 2500 - 16
= 2484
```

**Answer: 2484**

### 2. High Five Method

This method is used to quickly find the square of numbers ending in **5**.

**Example:**

```text
45²
= 4 × 5 | 25
= 20 | 25
= 2025
```

**Answer: 2025**

### 3. OZ Method

The OZ method provides a quick way to multiply numbers by **11**.

**Example:**

```text
63 × 11

6 | (6 + 3) | 3
= 6 | 9 | 3
= 693
```

**Answer: 693**

### 4. Minions Method

The Minions method can be used for suitable numbers that are close to convenient base values.

**Example:**

```text
79 × 80
= (80 - 1) × 80
= 6400 - 80
= 6320
```

**Answer: 6320**
